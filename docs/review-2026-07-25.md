# nira in-depth review — 2026-07-25

Seven parallel review passes (Dioxus reactivity, components, pages, CSS/design
system, a11y/keyboard, hooks/state, audio engine). Every finding cites
`file:line` and a concrete failure trace. `cargo check --workspace
--all-targets` is clean (exit 0, no warnings) including the uncommitted cover
overlay.

Findings marked **[verified]** were re-confirmed against source or a live
measurement after the reviewing pass reported them.

---

## Cross-cutting themes

Four patterns explain most of the ~40 findings.

**1. The shared `Signal<AppConfig>` is written on a hot path but read by
low-frequency effects.** Dioxus subscribes at whole-signal granularity, so a
volume drag fans out into network refetches, webview evals and config fsyncs.
Three independent passes found this from different directions.

**2. The global capture-phase keydown listener has too wide a blast radius.**
Binding bare Space and bare Arrow Up/Down at `document` with
`stopPropagation()` costs the app two universal keyboard primitives.

**3. CSS states intent that the render contradicts.** The concat-order cascade
in `main.rs:43-60` plus the blanket `box-shadow: none !important` mean edits at
a distance break things quietly, and nothing catches it.

**4. Good patterns exist but were not applied everywhere.** Pagination on 2 of
5 unbounded lists, `Arc` memo on 2 of 3 big vectors, error branches on 6 of 8
async surfaces, save-failure feedback on 4 of 9 config writes, load re-issue on
3 of 5 `current_index` mutations. Almost nothing here needs new abstraction —
it needs the existing pattern extended.

## P0 — user-visible, cheap to fix

### 1. Space does not activate any button in the app **[verified]**

`nira/src/main.rs:420-433`. The `typing(e)` guard at `:364` exempts
`input, textarea, select, [contenteditable]` — not `button`. The listener is
capture-phase on `document` and calls `stopPropagation()`, so the event never
reaches the focused button.

Repro: Tab to the sidebar "Settings" button, press Space → nothing; play/pause
fires instead. Same for Library tabs, theme buttons, every transport control.
Only Enter works anywhere.

Fix — one line before the letter block:
```js
if (isSpace && e.target && e.target.closest && e.target.closest('button:not(.hotkey-bridge)')) return;
```

### 2. Arrow keys are bound to volume, so nothing scrolls by keyboard **[verified]**

`nira/src/main.rs:427-428`. `.content` is the app's only scroll container
(`base.css:193`). Bare ↑/↓ are consumed with `preventDefault()` +
`stopPropagation()`.

Repro: Tab to any track row in Library, press ArrowDown → volume drops 5 %, the
list does not move. With Space also gone (finding 1), only PageUp/PageDown/
Home/End still scroll.

Fix: delete the two bare-arrow lines — `Ctrl+↑/↓` at `:415-416` already covers
volume — and drop bare ↑/↓ from `BINDS_VOLUME` at `components/src/hotkeys.rs:73-74`.

### 3. The queue popover's click-catcher covers the whole bottombar **[verified]**

`nira/assets/css/player.css:387-394` + `components/src/bottombar.rs:558-564`.
`.queue-overlay` is `position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:900`, rendered *inside*
`footer.player`, which is `position:absolute; z-index:10` — a stacking context.
Within it, 900 beats the unpositioned `.player-left/-center/-right`.

Repro: open the queue popover, click play/pause. The popover closes and
playback does **not** toggle. Every bottombar control needs two clicks while the
queue is open.

Fix:
```css
.player-left, .player-center, .player-right { position: relative; z-index: 902; }
```

### 4. Cover overlay is click-through for 400 ms while closing **[verified]**

`nira/assets/css/cover.css:24-28` sets `pointer-events: none` on `.closing`,
which makes the overlay *and all children* hit-transparent for the whole fade.

Repro: open the fullscreen cover over Home, double-click the backdrop (the
natural impatient dismiss). The second click lands on a track card underneath
and **starts playing that track**.

Fix: delete the `pointer-events: none;` line — `cover.rs:83-85` already guards
repeat closes in Rust.

### 5. Volume drag → per-tick network refetch, webview eval and config fsync **[verified]**

`hooks/src/use_player.rs:109` takes `config.write()` *before* the equality
check. `Signal::write()` marks subscribers dirty on guard drop regardless of
mutation, so even a no-op volume set dirties every `use_config()` subscriber.
Two effects read the whole `AppConfig`:

- `hooks/src/use_listenbrainz_feed.rs:38` — re-spawns a ListenBrainz fetch per
  tick (3 signal writes each). Network is spared by the 5-min TTL; the
  re-render storm is not.
- `nira/src/main.rs:322` — one `document::eval` round-trip per tick, writing
  `--font-ui` on `documentElement` (invalidates style for everything
  inheriting it), on the same thread servicing the drag.

Plus `save_bg` fires per integer step → one fsync of `config.json` per drag
pixel, queued ahead of any likes/playlist write the user makes next.

Fix (3 small diffs): compare `config.peek().volume` before taking the write
guard; add a `fetched_for` guard in the LB hook; hold the last-applied
`(ThemePref, String)` in the theme effect.

### 6. Volume control reads the 200-500 ms polled snapshot; unmute jumps loud

`components/src/bottombar.rs:83, 453`. `snapshot()` reads the *signal*, written
by a poller at 200/500 ms. `hotkeys.rs:190-193` does the opposite and says so in
a comment: *"config.volume is written synchronously by set_volume, so it's
always current — unlike the polled snapshot."* The M key and the speaker button
share a `MuteStash` but read different sources.

Repro: volume at 60 %. Drag to 5 %. Within ~200 ms click the speaker → stashes
**0.60**, mutes. Unmute → jumps back to 60 %.

Same root cause makes the slider fight the drag (`bottombar.rs:468`): the thumb
visibly snaps backwards mid-drag while playing.

Fix: `let config = use_config();` and read `config.read().volume` at both sites.

### 7. `.track-duration` — a documented WCAG fix is silently reverted **[verified]**

`tracks.css:98` sets `--text-muted` with the comment *"muted, not faint:
durations are data, and --faint fails WCAG AA contrast for text this size."*
`detail.css:447` re-declares it as `--text-faint` at identical specificity, and
`detail.css` loads 5 positions later (`main.rs:53` vs `:48`) — so it wins
**globally, on every page**.

Measured contrast: 2.33:1 (light) / 2.80:1 (dark), against the intended
4.89:1 / 6.33:1.

Fix: delete `detail.css:447-451`.

## P1 — correctness

### 8. Two queue mutations strand an in-flight load **[verified]**

`is_current_load` (`hooks/src/queue.rs:1061`) keys on the raw index, so *every*
`current_index` mutation invalidates a pending load. Three sites know this and
re-issue (`clear_upcoming:316`, `remove_at:406-414`, `toggle_shuffle:599-602`).
Two do not:

- `restore_pre_shuffle` `queue.rs:640-643` — the shuffle-OFF branch returns
  before reaching the guard at `:599`.
- `remove_at` `queue.rs:416-419` — the `idx < c` branch, directly below the
  `idx == c` branch that *does* re-issue with a comment.

Repro (A): Library → Shuffle. During the load (FLAC-first does a full the hi-res provider
search round-trip) hit the shuffle button to undo. `current_index` moves 0 → 17;
the in-flight task bails at commit. State: `advance_state == Loading`,
`is_loading_track == true`, no audio, title still showing, position blanked at
`0:00` forever. The watcher has no `Loading && !has_source` arm. Only Play or
Next recovers it.

Repro (B): click queue row 12, then remove row 3 during the load. Same wedge.

Fix: one helper called at both sites.
```rust
fn reissue_if_loading(&self) {
    if *self.advance_state.peek() == AdvanceState::Loading {
        self.bump_load_generation();
        load_current(self.clone());
    }
}
```
The structural fix is keying the load on entry URI + generation and dropping the
index from the identity — that makes all five sites correct by construction.

### 9. Enrichment cache: full-file fsync on the UI thread, per put, never evicted **[verified]**

`enrichment/src/cache.rs:92` calls `flush()` after every `put`, and `flush`
(`:124`) uses `atomic_write_json` — the **synchronous** path
(`config/src/lib.rs:387` → `write_synced` → `f.sync_all()`), under the
process-global `WRITE_LOCK`. Every other state file in the repo uses the `_bg`
variant. `put` is awaited from Dioxus tasks polled on the main thread, so the
fsync is inline on the UI thread.

The comment claims *"tiny in practice (~10KB after normal use)."* Measured on
this machine: **208,078 bytes** — 20× the estimate, because entries are only
overwritten if the same key recurs, and keys are per-recording.

Cost today: a Home "Refresh" issues up to ~42 puts × 208 KB = **~9 MB written
and 42 fsyncs on the UI thread per click**. Write volume is O(N²) in lookups and
the per-put stall grows without bound.

Fix (2 lines): `atomic_write_json_bg`. Bounding growth is separate — drop
entries past `self.ttl` while building the snapshot.

### 10. Spotify position extrapolates without bound, blinding the only stall detector

`player/src/spotify_backend.rs:158-175`. Position is `position_ms +
t.elapsed()` with no clamp.

Trace: network drops at 0:45. librespot's AP transport dies, no
`Paused`/`Stopped`/`EndOfTrack` event arrives, so `is_paused` stays false and
`has_track` stays true. Then:
- `queue.rs:1339-1353` detects stalls by `snap.position == prev`. Spotify's
  position *always* advances → structurally blind to every Spotify failure.
- `queue.rs:1417` auto-advances on `has_source == false`, which never clears.
- `session_is_invalid()` is checked only in `ensure_spotify`, i.e. only when the
  user starts a new track. Nothing polls it during playback.

Result: silence, a progress bar ticking past 100 %, no error, until the user
clicks something.

Fix: clamp the extrapolation to the known duration (`Player::snapshot` already
has it at `lib.rs:1059`) so the frozen position makes the existing detector
fire. Also `s.last_position_at = Some(Instant::now())` at `:172` is dead — `s`
is a returned clone.

### 11. Discover result rows are keyboard-dead

`pages/src/discover.rs:366-371` — no `tabindex`, no `role`, no `onkeydown`.
Every other track row in the app opts in (`parts.rs:130-131`,
`library.rs:1054-1055`, `bottombar.rs:655-656`). Tab skips every row; there is
no keyboard way to play a discovery result.

Fix: mirror `parts.rs:130-145` on the `li`.

### 12. No overlay contains or restores focus

`grep -rn 'inert\|aria-modal\|role: "dialog"'` over `components pages nira`
returns nothing. The visualizer (`viz.css:3`) and cover overlay
(`cover.css:10-14`) are fully opaque.

Repro A: press `V`, then Tab three times → focus is on sidebar/bottombar
controls behind an opaque canvas; the focus ring paints underneath and is
invisible. Enter activates something the user cannot see.

Repro B: right-click a row (the menu explicitly focuses the first item at
`ctx_menu.rs:105-110`), press Escape → focus falls to `<body>`. Tab restarts at
the sidebar. Worse: once focus is on `body`, the Rust shell handler at
`main.rs:453` stops receiving keydowns entirely.

Fix, per overlay: stash `document.activeElement` on open and `.focus()` it on
close. The ctx menu already has the eval plumbing.

### 13. ReplayGain applies up to 200 ms late at gapless boundaries, as a hard jump

`player/src/lib.rs:664-665`. `commit_gapless_if_crossed` runs only from
`snapshot()`, polled at 200 ms.

Trace: track A tagged `-10.5 dB` (0.30×), track B `0.0 dB` (1.0×). B starts and
plays at A's gain for up to 200 ms — 10 dB quiet — then jumps to 1.0× in one
sample. Reverse the tags and B's first 200 ms is 10 dB *too loud*. This is the
same class the unity-gain guard at `lib.rs:420-422` was written to kill,
reintroduced on the gapless path.

Fix: `pending_next` already carries `track_gain` — bake it into the appended
source (`.amplify(next/current)`) so it is correct before reaching the mixer,
and leave sink volume for the user slider.

### 14. A dead audio device freezes the UI permanently

`player/src/lib.rs:203-216`. nira never calls `with_error_callback`; rodio's
default only logs.

Trace: USB DAC unplugged mid-track. cpal stops invoking the data callback →
`periodic_access` stops ticking. User clicks the next track → `stop_for_load()`
→ `rodio.clear()` → `sleep_until_end.recv()` on a channel that will never fire
and never drop. **The Dioxus UI thread blocks forever.** Same for `try_seek`.
This also converts the deliberate ~10 ms lock hold at `lib.rs:922-930` into a
permanent one.

Related: `nira/src/main.rs:214-216` `.expect("audio engine failed to start")` —
autostart before PipeWire is up panics the whole app rather than starting muted.

Fix: `with_error_callback` setting an `AtomicBool`; check it before the blocking
rodio calls. Converts a hard hang into a surfaced error.

### 15. Progressive HTTP reads execute inside the cpal audio callback

`player/src/lib.rs:698-708`. `StreamDownload` is fed to `DecoderBuilder` and
appended to the rodio sink; `rodio-0.22.2/src/stream.rs:527` pulls
`samples.next()` from inside cpal's data callback. The 1 MiB prefetch is a
cushion (~7.5 s at the hi-res provider hi-res), not a fix — the comment shows the dropout was
observed and mitigated, not eliminated.

Worst case: the 30 s read timeout fires, the source ends, and
`queue.rs:1417-1434` treats it as a *natural end* and silently skips to the
next track mid-song. The user sees a track that ended early, no error.

Honest framing: fixing this properly means the decode-worker + PCM ring your
research doc describes, i.e. the architecture nira deliberately doesn't have.
The cheap win is distinguishing "source ended on reader error" from "track
finished" so auto-advance surfaces an error instead of skipping.

### 16. Three unbounded lists (pagination exists, just not here)

- `pages/src/library.rs:170` — Saved tab renders every liked track, **and it is

- `pages/src/library.rs:739` — Local "Singles" list. The album grid above it is
  capped; this is not. A 4,000-file folder with no ALBUM tags → ~3,800 rows.

### 17. Album page rebuilds the whole-library match index twice per render

`pages/src/album.rs:96` and `:234` each build a `HashSet` over `local.tracks`;
`track_match_key` is 8 allocations per track. Both read a ROOT-scope signal, so
they stay subscribed while hidden under `display:none` in the detail stack.

Trace: 15,000-track library, stack is artist→album→artist→album. A the hi-res provider
download finishes → rescan → 6 index builds × 15,000 × 8 ≈ **720,000
allocations on the UI thread**, five-sixths for invisible pages.

Fix: one `use_memo` in `AlbumPage`, passed to both children.

### 18. The detail stack is unbounded

`hooks/src/use_detail.rs:49` guards only against re-pushing the top.
artist→album→artist→album is a two-levels-per-lap loop with no ceiling, and
`MainContent` mounts one div per level. 20 minutes of related-artist browsing ≈
30 mounted pages, each holding a live view and its rendered grid. Only `back()`
one at a time or a sidebar click frees them. Multiplies finding 17.

Fix: `if s.len() >= 12 { s.remove(0); }` before pushing.

## What is genuinely good

Worth recording, because several passes went looking for bugs the comments had
already anticipated and fixed.

- **Dioxus hygiene is better than most 0.7 codebases.** The `read`/`peek`
  discipline is deliberate and near-universal. Zero borrow-panic risks: the
  queue drops guards before `jump_to`, `set_volume` scopes its write,
  `load_related` documents its peek-clone-set. Zero effect loops — every effect
  that writes a signal it could depend on reads it through `peek()`.
- **All twelve early-`return rsx!` sites have their hooks above the return.**
  Checked individually.
- **The staleness model is right and consistently applied.** Monotonic
  generation counters checked after every await, across six independent async
  surfaces, rather than relying on task cancellation. `use_resource` is absent
  deliberately — its restart semantics couldn't express this.
- **The scrub-hold state machine is the most carefully-reasoned code in the
  repo**, and the two copies (bottombar/cover) have *not* diverged — all six
  behavioural branches traced identical.
- **`spawn` vs `spawn_forever` is decided per call site with a rationale**, not
  by blanket rule. HTTP timeouts are honoured in all five clients, with a
  comment explaining why a *total* timeout is wrong for streaming.
